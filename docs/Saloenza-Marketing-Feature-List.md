## 1. What is Saloenza?

Saloenza is a **cloud-based salon and spa management platform** that helps beauty businesses run their day-to-day operations from one place — and helps Saloenza (as a software company) sell and manage those salons as customers.

### In simple terms

| For… | Saloenza helps them… |
|------|----------------------|
| **Salon owners** | Run one or many branches, manage staff, appointments, sales, stock, and reports |
| **Branch managers** | Run the floor — bookings, walk-ins, clients, inventory, and team |
| **Stylists / front desk** | See their schedule, live queue, clients, and own earnings |
| **Salon clients (customers)** | Book online via a shareable link, get reminders, and receive receipts |
| **Affiliate partners** | Refer salons and earn commissions |
| **Saloenza platform admins** | Onboard salons, manage plans, approvals, and partner payouts |

### What problems it solves

- Missed appointments and messy paper diaries  
- Walk-in chaos at the front desk  
- No clear view of which stylist is free  
- Lost client history and contact details  
- Retail product stock surprises  
- Unclear revenue, unpaid bills, and month-end numbers  
- Growing from one shop to multiple branches without losing control  
- Selling the software itself (plans, renewals, upgrades, partners)

## 2. Who Uses Saloenza? (All Roles)

Saloenza has **three worlds** of users: the salon team, customers (without a full login), and the Saloenza platform / partners.

### 2.1 Salon team roles

| Role | Plain-language description | Typical access |
|------|----------------------------|----------------|
| **Salon Franchise Owner** | The business owner. Full control of the salon across all branches. | Everything: branches, staff, roles, catalog, bookings, POS, queue, customers, inventory, expenses, reports, billing, referrals, settings |
| **Franchise Manager** | Owner’s right hand across the whole salon. | Almost everything the owner can do, except permanently deleting sensitive records (create / view / update) |
| **Branch Manager** | Runs one location day to day. | Front desk + that branch’s staff, catalog updates, inventory, expenses, booking links, full live queue, customer contacts, analytics for that scope |
| **Staff** (stylist / front desk) | Delivers services and handles day-to-day bookings. | View services/products; create and update appointments; manage customers; see **own** earnings; see **own** live queue. No inventory, reports, roles, or billing by default |

**Extra flexibility:** Owners and managers can create **custom roles** and decide exactly what each role can see and do (for example: “Reception only” or “Senior stylist”).

**Hierarchy (highest → lowest):** Owner → Franchise Manager → Branch Manager → Staff

### 2.2 Customers (salon clients)

Customers are stored as **client records** in the salon’s database.  
They do **not** need a Saloenza login account.

They interact by:

- Booking through a **public booking link** shared by the salon  
- Receiving **email / SMS** confirmations, reminders, and occasion wishes  
- Getting a **PDF receipt** after a visit or purchase  

### 2.3 Affiliate Partner

External growth partners who:

1. Apply publicly  
2. Get approved by Saloenza admin  
3. Refer new salons  
4. Track commissions  
5. Request withdrawals  

### 2.4 Platform Super Admin (Saloenza internal)

Runs the software business:

- Onboards and manages salons  
- Manages subscription plans, renewals, upgrades, and history  
- Approves affiliates and payouts  
- Sets platform-wide branding and default settings  
- Views platform reports and admin notifications

## 3. How a Salon Starts — End-to-End Onboarding

### Flow A — Salon signs up themselves

1. Owner **registers** (email or phone).  
2. Completes an **onboarding wizard**: create account → set up first branch → add services → finish.  
3. Starts on a **Free** setup plan (or chooses a paid plan).  
4. If paying offline (bank / UPI / cash), waits for **activation** after Saloenza confirms payment.  
5. Invites staff, sets branding, and goes live.

### Flow B — Saloenza admin onboards the salon

1. Super Admin creates the salon in **Salon Onboarding**.  
2. Owner account is prepared / completed.  
3. Plan is assigned (Free / Basic / Pro / Enterprise).  
4. Owner logs in and finishes branch, catalog, and staff setup.  
5. Salon begins taking appointments and walk-ins.

### After go-live (first week checklist for marketing demos)

- Add branches (if multi-location)  
- Upload logo and brand colors  
- Create categories, services, and retail products  
- Add staff and assign roles  
- Create a **booking link** and share it  
- Turn on appointment reminders and birthday wishes  
- Configure payment and invoice preferences

## 4. Module-by-Module Feature List

Each module below includes: **what it is**, **who uses it**, **what you can do**, and a short **day-in-the-life flow**.

---

### 4.1 Dashboard

**What it is:** The home screen after login — a quick picture of “what’s happening today.”

**Who uses it:** Owner, managers, staff, platform admin (each sees a role-appropriate view).

**What you can do**

- See today’s appointments and key activity at a glance  
- Jump quickly to queue, appointments, customers, or admin tasks  
- Platform admin sees pending onboarding, affiliate applications, and upgrade requests  

**Simple flow:** Login → open Dashboard → click into the area that needs attention.

---

### 4.2 Appointments & Calendar

**What it is:** The salon diary — schedule services, assign stylists, and track visit status.

**Who uses it:** Owner, franchise manager, branch manager, staff (as permitted).

**What you can do**

- View appointments in calendar or list form  
- Book multi-service visits and assign staff  
- Track status: **Scheduled → Confirmed → In progress → Completed** (or Cancelled / No-show)  
- Support different visit types: appointment, walk-in, product sale  
- Track booking source: internal, POS, walk-in, or online self-booking  
- Apply discounts where needed  
- Check staff availability and avoid double-booking conflicts  
- Capture payment, refund, or send a payment reminder  
- Download a **PDF receipt**

**End-to-end flow**

1. Reception or stylist creates a booking (or client books online).  
2. Client gets confirmation (email/SMS if enabled).  
3. Reminder goes out before the visit.  
4. Staff starts the service from calendar or live queue.  
5. Service is completed.  
6. Payment is taken (cash / card / UPI / bank / other).  
7. Receipt is shared or downloaded.  
8. Visit appears in customer history and reports.

---

### 4.3 Live Queue

**What it is:** A floor-friendly view of who is waiting, who is in service, and who is coming next.

**Who uses it**

- Staff — primarily their own chair / own queue  
- Managers — full branch queue (when permitted)

**What you can see / do**

- Buckets such as: **In service**, **Waiting**, **Upcoming**  
- Walk-in visibility  
- Start and complete services from the queue context  

**End-to-end flow**

1. Day starts; booked clients appear as upcoming.  
2. Near arrival time, they move into waiting.  
3. Stylist starts service.  
4. Stylist completes service.  
5. Payment is completed if still unpaid.

**Plan note:** Included from **Basic** plan upward.

---

### 4.4 Online Booking Links (Customer Self-Booking)

**What it is:** A shareable web link so clients can book without creating a Saloenza account.

**Who uses it**

- Managers / owners create and manage links  
- Customers use the public page `/book/...`

**What you can do**

- Create booking links for the salon / branch  
- Activate or deactivate a link  
- Let customers choose services and available time slots  
- Collect name, phone, email, and notes  

**End-to-end flow**

1. Manager creates a booking link.  
2. Salon shares the link (WhatsApp, Instagram bio, SMS, website, QR).  
3. Customer opens the page and picks service + slot.  
4. Booking is confirmed and appears on the salon calendar / queue.  
5. Reminder and visit flow continue as a normal appointment.

---

### 4.5 POS (Point of Sale) / Walk-in Checkout

**What it is:** Front-desk checkout for walk-ins and retail product sales.

**Who uses it:** Front desk and staff with appointment access.

**What you can do**

- Browse the service and product catalog  
- Build a cart with multiple services and/or products  
- Check out and take payment immediately  
- Complete walk-ins and product sales in one step  
- Automatically reduce stock when retail products are sold  

**End-to-end flow**

1. Walk-in client arrives (or client wants to buy a product).  
2. Staff opens POS and adds items.  
3. Checkout and payment.  
4. Sale is marked complete.  
5. Stock updates (for products).  
6. Receipt is available; sale shows in reports.

---

### 4.6 Customers (Client CRM)

**What it is:** The salon’s client database and relationship history.

**Who uses it:** Staff and above; full contact visibility typically for managers+.

**What you can do**

- Add, edit, and deactivate clients  
- Store phone, email, notes  
- Record birthday and anniversary  
- Tag / segment clients  
- See visit and appointment history  
- Jump to clients from reports  

**Automated goodwill**

- Daily **birthday / anniversary wish** emails (with optional offer text configured by the salon)

**End-to-end flow**

1. New client books or walks in → profile is created or matched.  
2. Every visit adds to history.  
3. Staff can personalize service using notes and tags.  
4. On special occasions, Saloenza can send a branded wish / offer.

---

### 4.7 Catalog — Categories, Services & Products

**What it is:** The menu of everything the salon sells.

**Who uses it**

- Owners / managers manage the catalog  
- Staff view it for booking and POS  

**What you can do**

- Organize **categories** (e.g., Hair, Skin, Nails, Retail)  
- Define **services** with duration and price  
- Link products / consumables used by a service  
- Define **retail products** for sale and stock  
- Enable offerings and pricing at salon level  

**End-to-end flow**

1. Owner sets up categories.  
2. Adds services and products with prices.  
3. Booking, POS, inventory, and reports all use this same catalog.

---

### 4.8 Inventory, Suppliers & Purchase Orders

**What it is:** Stock control for retail products and service consumables.

**Who uses it:** Branch managers and owners (inventory permissions).

**What you can do**

- See stock levels by branch  
- Adjust stock and view movement history  
- Manage **suppliers**  
- Create **purchase orders**: draft → place → receive (or cancel)  
- View product sales related to stock  

**Screens typically include:** Stock | Purchase Orders | Product Sales

**End-to-end flow**

1. Stock runs low (or staff notices shortage).  
2. Manager creates a purchase order to a supplier.  
3. Order is placed.  
4. Goods are received → stock increases.  
5. POS / completed sales reduce stock again as items are sold.

**Plan note:** Included from **Basic** plan upward.

---

### 4.9 Staff Management

**What it is:** Team directory and basic work profile for each person.

**Who uses it:** Owners and managers.

**What you can do**

- Add / edit / remove staff  
- Assign roles and branch  
- Add photo, commission rate, monthly salary field  
- Maintain weekly schedule on the staff profile  
- Respect plan limits on maximum staff count  

**Related: My Earnings**

- Staff can view their own earnings / work from completed and in-progress appointments (commission-oriented view).

**End-to-end flow**

1. Owner adds a stylist and assigns “Staff” role to a branch.  
2. Stylist logs in and sees own schedule, queue, and earnings.  
3. Completed services contribute to earnings visibility for that staff member.

---

### 4.10 Roles & Permissions

**What it is:** Security controls — decide who can see or change what.

**Who uses it:** Owners and managers with roles access.

**What you can do**

- View system roles  
- Create custom roles  
- Clone roles  
- Assign permissions module by module  
- Protect system roles from unsafe changes  

**End-to-end flow**

1. Owner creates a custom role (example: “Reception”).  
2. Allows appointments + customers; blocks inventory and billing.  
3. Assigns the role to new front-desk hires.

**Plan note:** Available on **Free**, and again on **Pro / Enterprise** (not on Basic in the seeded packaging).

---

### 4.11 Branches (Multi-Location)

**What it is:** Support for salons with more than one shop / location.

**Who uses it:** Owners / franchise managers; branch managers work inside their location; platform admin can manage capacity across salons.

**What you can do**

- Create and update branches  
- Scope staff and operations to a branch  
- Stay within plan limits (e.g., Free/Basic = 1 branch, Pro = 3, Enterprise = unlimited)

**End-to-end flow**

1. Franchise owner adds a second branch.  
2. Assigns a branch manager and staff.  
3. That location runs its own appointments, queue, stock, and clients within the same salon account.

---

### 4.12 Analytics, Reports & Expenses

**What it is:** Business visibility — how the salon is performing financially and operationally.

**Who uses it:** Owners and managers with analytics access.

**What you can see / do**

- Summary KPIs  
- Revenue and appointment trends  
- Top services  
- Staff performance overview  
- Payment status and payment method breakdown  
- **Profit & Loss** (including expenses)  
- **Outstanding payments** list + reminders  
- **Business close / ledger** style reporting  
- Create and manage **expenses** and expense categories  

**End-to-end month-end flow**

1. Collect outstanding client payments.  
2. Log operating expenses.  
3. Review P&L and business close report.  
4. Check staff earnings / commission picture.  
5. Decide pricing, staffing, or stock actions for next month.

**Plan note:** Typically **Pro / Enterprise**.

---

### 4.13 Salon Billing (Paying for Saloenza)

**What it is:** How the salon pays for the Saloenza software subscription.

**Who uses it:** Salon owners (settings / billing access).

**What you can do**

- View current plan and expiry  
- Upgrade or renew  
- Pay online via **Razorpay** or **Stripe**  
- Or request **manual / offline** upgrade (bank / UPI / cash) for admin approval  
- When Free trial expires, portal can lock to billing-only until upgraded  
- When a paid plan expires, access can become read-only until renewed  

**End-to-end flow**

1. Owner opens Billing.  
2. Chooses a higher plan.  
3. Pays online **or** submits offline proof / request.  
4. Admin approves if offline.  
5. Modules unlock according to the new plan.

---

### 4.14 Settings & Branding

**What it is:** Make Saloenza look and behave like *your* salon.

**Who uses it:** Salon owners (and platform defaults from Super Admin).

**Salon settings include**

| Area | Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Profile** | Name, photo, password |
| **Business profile** | Salon identity, GST, WhatsApp number, support contacts |
| **Branding** | Portal name, logo, primary/secondary colors |
| **Email** | From name, SMTP / delivery setup |
| **SMS** | Provider choice (platform default, AWS SNS, MSG91, Twilio) |
| **Notifications** | Confirmations, reminders, cancellations, staff alerts, renewal notices, outstanding payment reminders, birthday/anniversary wishes + offer text |
| **Regional** | Timezone, language/locale, currency (e.g. QAR / USD), date & time formats |
| **Invoice** | Number prefix, tax label, footer text, show GST |
| **Branches** | Manage locations (where permitted) |
| **Billing** | Plan management |

**How settings are decided:** Salon-specific settings override platform defaults, which override system defaults.

## 5. End-to-End Business Flows

These are the stories marketing can tell in demos and brochures.

### Flow 1 — New salon joins Saloenza

**Register / Admin onboard → Configure branch & services → Choose plan → Pay or activate → Invite staff → Go live**

Outcome: Salon is ready to take bookings the same week.

---

### Flow 2 — Client books online and visits

**Share booking link → Client picks service & slot → Confirmed booking → Reminder → Arrival → Live queue → Service → Pay → PDF receipt**

Outcome: Less phone tag; fuller chairs; professional client experience.

---

### Flow 3 — Busy walk-in / retail day

**POS cart (services and/or products) → Pay → Stock updates → Sale in reports → Receipt**

Outcome: Front desk never loses a sale because the diary and stock book are disconnected.

---

### Flow 4 — Restock products

**Low stock noticed → Create purchase order → Place with supplier → Receive goods → Stock up → Continue selling**

Outcome: Fewer “sorry, out of stock” moments.

---

### Flow 5 — Month-end business review

**Chase outstanding payments → Log expenses → Run P&L / business close → Review staff earnings → Plan next month**

Outcome: Owner knows profit, not just “busy-ness.”

---

### Flow 6 — Grow the network

**Salon refers another salon for credits** *or* **Affiliate refers a salon → Commission accrues → Withdrawal requested → Admin marks paid**

Outcome: Organic growth engine for Saloenza and rewards for partners/owners.

---

### Flow 7 — Subscription upgrade when the salon grows

**Hit limits or need analytics → Upgrade plan → Online or offline payment → Modules unlock (queue, inventory, reports, more branches/staff)**

Outcome: Product grows with the salon instead of forcing a tool switch.
